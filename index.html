Submissions are open!

All submissions are due Friday April 30th, 2027 at 3pm.

Submit your short film via the 2027 Submission Form⇗⇗. Eligible films must have been created after September 1, 2026 by a full-time Connecticut high school student and be 6 minutes or less in runtime.
